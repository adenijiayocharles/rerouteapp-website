ADOPT, DON'T FIGHT

re:route learns your setup — it doesn't fight it.

A first-launch scan finds hand-added entries and lets you choose which to bring under management. Every write after that round-trips everything else byte-for-byte — other tools' entries, comments, and blank lines are left exactly as they were.
